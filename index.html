How to Back Up & Migrate Your Data

Because your data never touches a remote server, follow these simple steps to safeguard your records.

JSON Full Backup & Device Migration

Use this to move your complete setup (clients, shifts, invoices, certs) to another browser, phone, or laptop.

  1. Click "JSON Backup" in the top header. A .json file will immediately download to your device.
  2. Email this downloaded file to yourself, or upload it to your Google Drive / personal cloud.
  3. On your new computer, phone, or secondary browser, open the SupportHub web app.
  4. Click "Restore JSON" in the top header, select your saved .json file, and all your records will instantly load into place.
CSV Spreadsheet Archival

Use this for tax time, vehicle travel audits, or long-term record-keeping in Google Sheets or Excel.

  1. Click "Export Shifts CSV" in the top header to download your entire shift log.
  2. Save the file into your local records folder or your personal Google Drive / OneDrive.
  3. Open Google Sheets, create a new spreadsheet, click File > Import > Upload, and select your CSV file.
  4. Your shifts, hourly calculations, km, and totals will be formatted into a permanent, searchable spreadsheet archive.

What is SupportHub?

An all-in-one local dashboard engineered specifically for disability support workers, independent contractors, carers, and sole traders.

Who is it for?

Independent support providers, agency casuals working across multiple providers, and participants self-managing care teams.

100% Offline & Private Local Storage

Unlike cloud databases that store sensitive participant details on remote third-party servers, SupportHub runs exclusively in your browser. All client rosters, emergency contacts, handover memos, and invoices remain on your physical device.

Key Features:

  • Interactive calendar shift logging & completion toggles
  • Dedicated single-client invoicing studio & archive
  • PDF, PNG, and JPEG instant document downloads
  • Multi-employer & agency rate directory
  • Client sensory protocols & ICE contacts
  • Clearances & compliance expiry monitor
Usage Disclaimer & Data Backup Responsibility

SupportHub Control Panel is provided for organizational convenience and does not warrant an official legal, medical, or tax accounting record. Use of this application is at the user's sole discretion and liability. Because data is kept purely on your local browser, it is strictly the user's responsibility to routinely download backups using the "JSON Backup" and "Shifts CSV" buttons to avoid accidental loss if browser cache is cleared.
